Food as
Medicine

Let Food Be the Foundation

Long before modern medicine, food was our first and most trusted form of healing. We honor that wisdom by sharing it — teaching how everyday foods and herbs can calm, strengthen, and restore the body — and by growing fresh, nourishing food on our land to reach the communities that need it most. Here, food as medicine isn't a regimen handed to you. It's knowledge you can carry into your own kitchen, and nourishment we work to make accessible to all.

Workshops & Classes

Learn to use food and herbs as medicine — open to guests and community members.

Food as Medicine Classes

Group sessions on how the foods you eat can calm inflammation, support recovery, and restore balance — with practical guidance to use at home.

Culinary Medicine Workshops

Hands-on cooking classes where you learn to prepare simple, healing meals from whole, seasonal ingredients.

Herbal Workshops

Learn to grow, harvest, and prepare medicinal herbs, taught by practitioners and rooted in traditional knowledge.

Seasonal Eating Workshops

Discover how to cook and eat with the rhythm of the seasons, using produce grown on our own land.

Community Food Access

Fresh, locally grown food brought to homes and communities.

Farm Produce Shares

A seasonal share of the farm's harvest for individuals and families — fresh vegetables and healing herbs, available for pickup or delivered to your door.

Veggie Van

Fresh, locally grown produce carried directly into communities where good food is hard to find, and shared at no cost with the families and neighbors who need it most.
